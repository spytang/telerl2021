\begin{abstract}
5G 无线接入网（RAN）需要在同一物理基础设施上同时承载增强型移动宽带（eMBB）与超可靠低时延通信（URLLC）等不一样的业务。
URLLC 的突发流量通常需要通过微时隙（Mini-slot）级抢占立即获得资源，但这样的过程会对正在传输的 eMBB 码字产生打孔（Puncturing）损伤。
如何在满足 URLLC 严格时延约束的同时降低 eMBB 中断风险，是混合业务的网络切片的调度中的核心问题。
围绕这个问题，我在本文构建了一个与 5G NR Slot/Mini-slot 时序对齐的单小区 Gymnasium 仿真环境，
并将 URLLC/eMBB 动态抢占建模为带有理想可靠性裕度观测的容忍度感知马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）。

我在本文的主要贡献包括了三个的方面。
首先，我设计了包含 URLLC 队列状态、剩余时限、时隙位置、历史打孔计数、eMBB 码字理想可靠性裕度及剩余裕度等信息的 rich observation，
使得强化学习智能体能够在 MAC 层调度中显式感知物理层打孔风险。其次，我在近端策略优化（PPO）基础上提出频域打孔负载均衡策略 VarPPO，这样的策略可以在基线
时延违约与中断惩罚之外，引入打孔计数方差、剩余可靠性裕度方差、码字可用状态方差和滚动吞吐方差等风险规避项，
引导智能体避免将 URLLC 抢占压力长期集中到少数频域资源上。最后，我在本文建立了覆盖静态抢占、确定性轮询、基线 PPO 与 VarPPO 的统一评估流程，
并进一步设计分布外（OOD）URLLC 到达率扫频测试，用来考察策略在突发负载迁移下的鲁棒性边界。

在单小区、Perfect-CSI 辅助观测的仿真实验中，VarPPO 在保持 URLLC 违约率与其他策略基本持平的条件下，主要改善了 eMBB 侧可靠性与风险分布。
在基础到达率 \(\lambda=0.5\) 下，VarPPO 相比基线 PPO 将公共惩罚幅度降低约 \(5.8\%\)，将 eMBB outage rate 降低约 \(18.0\%\)，并将剩余可靠性裕度方差降低约 \(14.8\%\)；在高负载 OOD 测试中，VarPPO 在 \(\lambda=1.0,1.2,1.5\) 时也保持了低于 PPO 的 eMBB 中断率。本文同时估算了轻量级 Actor 网络的单次前向传播开销约为 \(1.75\times10^4\) FLOPs，说明该类策略具备进一步映射到 O-RAN 近实时控制器或边缘推理模块进行验证的潜力。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
Future radio access networks must support heterogeneous services with conflicting requirements on a shared physical infrastructure. In mixed eMBB and URLLC slicing, URLLC packets may require immediate mini-slot-level preemption, yet every punctured resource block can weaken an ongoing eMBB codeword and, when the accumulated damage exceeds its tolerance, trigger a service outage. How can a scheduler react quickly to URLLC bursts without repeatedly sacrificing the same vulnerable eMBB resources? To study this latency-reliability tension in a controlled setting, this thesis builds a custom Gymnasium simulation environment aligned with the 5G NR slot/mini-slot structure and formulates the preemptive scheduling task as a tolerance-aware Markov Decision Process with oracle-assisted reliability-margin observations. The oracle information is not intended as a directly deployable assumption; instead, it provides an upper-bound experimental setting for examining how risk-aware deep reinforcement learning behaves when the scheduler can explicitly perceive puncturing tolerance.

The work develops a reproducible single-cell downlink simulation framework that captures the research conditions observed during implementation: URLLC packets arrive according to a Poisson process, their deadlines are measured at mini-slot granularity, preemption decisions are made over discrete frequency-domain resources, and eMBB codewords evolve from healthy transmission states to outage states as puncturing accumulates. Within this setting, the observation space combines queue state, remaining deadlines, slot timing, puncturing history, ideal eMBB reliability margins, residual margins, outage indicators, and previous-step feedback, so that the learning agent can associate short-term URLLC urgency with longer-term eMBB risk. Building on PPO, this thesis proposes VarPPO, a scheduling policy that adds frequency-domain puncturing load balancing to the usual penalties for URLLC deadline violations and eMBB outages. Its variance penalties on puncturing counts, residual reliability margins, codeword availability, and rolling throughput translate risk-averse resource allocation into the discrete preemption scenario, encouraging the agent to spread puncturing pressure instead of exploiting a small subset of apparently safe resources. The evaluation compares these learned policies with static and round-robin heuristics as well as vanilla PPO, covering both the training traffic distribution and out-of-distribution URLLC arrival rates so that robustness degradation under burstier traffic can be observed directly.

The experiments therefore ask not only whether VarPPO can reduce the average cost, but also whether it changes the reliability profile seen by the eMBB slice. In the single-cell Perfect-CSI-assisted setting, the main improvement appears on the eMBB side while URLLC latency performance remains comparable to the baselines. At the base arrival rate 
\(\lambda=0.5\), VarPPO reduces the common evaluation penalty by about \(5.8\%\), the eMBB outage rate by about \(18.0\%\), and the residual-margin variance by about \(14.8\%\) compared with vanilla PPO. Under heavier out-of-distribution loads, it also maintains lower eMBB outage rates than PPO at \(\lambda=1.0,1.2,\) and \(1.5\), although both methods eventually approach the same URLLC capacity boundary once arrivals exceed the one-packet-per-mini-slot service limit. The deployed Actor network requires approximately \(1.75\times10^4\) FLOPs per forward pass, suggesting that the proposed framework can serve as a practical basis for further O-RAN xApp-style policy validation and edge inference studies in intelligent RAN slicing.
\end{abstract*}
