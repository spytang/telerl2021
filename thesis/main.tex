\begin{abstract}
随着 5G 网络中增强型移动宽带（Enhanced Mobile Broadband, eMBB）与超可靠低时延通信（Ultra-Reliable Low-Latency Communication, URLLC）等异构业务的并发接入，如何在有限无线资源条件下实现高效、稳定且满足服务等级协议约束的切片资源分配，已成为无线接入网资源管理中的关键问题。针对传统固定调度策略难以适应动态流量波动、优化方法在复杂环境下难以实时求解的问题，本文以单小区下行场景为研究对象，基于自建 Gymnasium 仿真环境，构建了面向 URLLC/eMBB 混合切片的资源分配模型，并引入深度强化学习方法进行优化。

本文将切片资源分配过程建模为具有容忍度感知观测的马尔可夫决策过程（tolerance-aware Markov Decision Process, MDP）。环境支持 \texttt{minimal} 与 \texttt{rich} 两种观测模式，其中 PPO 与 VarPPO 均采用包含 URLLC 队列、时延直方图、时隙位置、子载波打孔计数、eMBB 码字可靠性裕度估计及历史反馈的 \texttt{rich} 观测。本文设计了统一的公共评分函数以刻画 URLLC 违约与 eMBB 中断，并在 VarPPO 中进一步加入负载、剩余裕度、码字吞吐及滚动吞吐的方差惩罚。实验部分设置 \texttt{fixed\_static}、\texttt{fixed\_rr}、\texttt{PPO} 和 \texttt{VarPPO} 四组对比方案，并从训练收敛性、公共评分、方差分布以及负载迁移鲁棒性等方面进行评估。结果分析以公共评分为主要公平比较口径；训练奖励曲线仅用于观察各自学习过程，因为 VarPPO 的训练奖励额外扣除了方差惩罚。

本文的研究为 5G RAN 切片资源分配提供了一种可复现的仿真框架，也为后续向 O-RAN xApp 部署与更复杂场景扩展奠定了基础。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
With the coexistence of heterogeneous services such as enhanced mobile broadband (eMBB) and ultra-reliable low-latency communication (URLLC) in 5G networks, efficient and stable slice-level resource allocation under limited radio resources has become a major challenge in radio access network management. Traditional heuristic scheduling policies often fail to adapt to highly dynamic traffic, while optimization-based methods are difficult to solve in real time under complex environments. To address this issue, this thesis investigates a single-cell downlink scenario and develops a Gymnasium-based simulation environment for URLLC/eMBB mixed slicing resource allocation.

The resource allocation problem is formulated as a tolerance-aware Markov decision process with CSI-assisted observations. The simulator supports both \texttt{minimal} and \texttt{rich} observation modes, while PPO and VarPPO are trained with the \texttt{rich} observation that includes URLLC queue information, deadline statistics, slot/minislot timing, per-subcarrier puncture counts, estimated eMBB reliability margins, outage flags, and previous-step feedback. A common score is used to fairly measure URLLC violations and eMBB outages, and VarPPO further subtracts variance/load-balancing penalties over puncture counts, remaining margins, per-codeword throughput, and rolling throughput. Four schemes are evaluated, including \texttt{fixed\_static}, \texttt{fixed\_rr}, \texttt{PPO}, and \texttt{VarPPO}. The main comparison relies on the shared common score rather than directly comparing PPO and VarPPO training rewards, because VarPPO optimizes an intentionally penalized reward.

This work provides a reproducible simulation framework for 5G RAN slicing resource allocation and lays the foundation for future deployment in O-RAN xApps and more realistic network scenarios.
\end{abstract*}
